Common Questions

Is there a fee to request a book?
No — requesting is completely free. You only pay if we find the book and you decide to order it.
How long does sourcing take?
We usually respond within 24–48 hours. If we find it, delivery typically takes 3–7 business days depending on your location.
What if you can't find the book?
We'll let you know honestly. We won't keep you waiting — if we can't source it we'll tell you straight away.
Can I request textbooks or academic books?
Yes! We do our best to source academic and educational titles too. Just provide the ISBN or edition details if you have them.
